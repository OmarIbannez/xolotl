### 1.3 KYC minimums
- ID: cédula (natural persons), RUC (legal entities), passport (foreigners). Identity check against **Registro Civil (Dirección General de Registro Civil, Identificación y Cedulación)** — DINARDAP data-sharing platform; biometric validation services available to FIs. UNVERIFIED.
- Remote onboarding: permitted for fintechs under Ley Fintech framework; specific biometric/liveness rules from SB/JPRF not located — GAP.
- UBO: **10%** ownership/voting (SRI REBEFICS definition); FIU rules commonly use 10%+ too. SECONDARY — https://nmslaw.com.ec/blog/2024/09/23/reporte-beneficiarios-finales-composicion-societaria-ecuador/ . National UBO register = **REBEFICS at SRI** (not public).
- PEP: defined in Reglamento (domestic/foreign/IO, close associates, family to 2nd degree consanguinity/affinity) — UNVERIFIED detail. UAFE publishes PEP position list (UNVERIFIED).
- Retention: **10 years** (AML law) — UNVERIFIED.

### 1.4 Screening & freeze
- UN Security Council consolidated list (mandatory, TF/PF); new 2026 Reglamento enables administrative fund immobilisation (SECONDARY). OFAC widely used by banks (dollarized system, correspondent pressure). Local lists: UAFE notifications; Ecuador designated "grupos armados organizados"/terrorist groups (Los Choneros, Los Lobos designated terrorist by US in 2025) — UNVERIFIED.

### 1.5 Recent / upcoming changes
- Jan-2026 new Reglamento + CONCLAFT + Plan 2026-2030. Apr-2026 Res. UAFE-DG-2026-0007 (ROS 5 days, NO-ROS monthly by day 10). Aug-2025 RUC-UAFE linkage. 5th-round GAFILAT evaluation upcoming.

### 1.6 Gaps
- RESU file layout/XSD and SISLAFT technical manual not retrieved (UAFE site). Exact retention article and remote-onboarding rules unconfirmed. VASP definition remains thin; no prudential licence.

### 2.3 KYC minimums
- ID: cédula de identidad / DNI digital (DNIC – Dirección Nacional de Identificación Civil, Ministerio del Interior); DNI with chip supports electronic identity (ID Uruguay / AGESIC). Due diligence per RNCSF arts. ~290-300s (normal, simplified, enhanced). UNVERIFIED article numbers.
- Remote onboarding: BCU allows non-face-to-face onboarding for IEDE/FIs with identity validation (DNIC biometric/electronic ID); exact article not confirmed — GAP.
- UBO: **15%** of capital/voting or control (Ley 19.484 / Ley 19.574); national UBO register held by **BCU** (non-public, accessible by UIAF, DGI, Fiscalía). UNVERIFIED (high confidence from background).
- PEP: Ley 19.574 art. 20 – domestic, foreign & IO PEPs, kept PEP status **5 years** after leaving office; family & close associates. UNVERIFIED.
- Retention: **5 years** minimum (Ley 19.574 art. 21) — UNVERIFIED.

### 2.4 Screening & freeze
- UN Security Council lists (Ley 19.749 of 2019 on TF/PF targeted financial sanctions — UNVERIFIED number): immediate freeze and report to UIAF. UIAF also circulates lists; OFAC used commercially. UIAF administrative blocking now up to 5 business days (Ley 20.469).

### 2.5 Recent / upcoming changes
- Mar-2026 Ley 20.469 (cash cap 200k UI, blocking 5 business days); Jun-2026 IEDE systematic reporting; Jul-2026 PSAV final regime (Circular 2507) — authorisation window 1-Sep-2026 → 31-Mar-2027; National Strategy 2025-2030. GAFILAT 5th-round evaluation expected ~2027-2028 (UNVERIFIED).

### 2.6 Gaps
